Notes
- Past: nothing in the window has passed except Deepavali, which is **today**.
- Facts we may quote (verbatim from the opened pages):
  - Diabetes (WHO): "More than 40% of people living with diabetes are undiagnosed." Common symptoms: increased thirst, frequent urination, tiredness, blurred vision. Type 2 usually develops gradually and may cause few or no symptoms.
  - Preterm (WHO): "Around 1 in 10 babies worldwide is preterm – born before 37 weeks of pregnancy." WHO antenatal guidance: "a minimum of 8 contacts with health professionals throughout pregnancy – starting before 12 weeks". Kangaroo mother care and early breastfeeding are among WHO's recommended interventions for preterm babies.
  - Cervical cancer (WHO): almost all cases are caused by HPV; HPV vaccination is a priority for girls aged 9–14; regular screening can find changes before they become cancer; "Cervical cancer can be cured if diagnosed and treated at an early stage of disease." Vaccination does not replace screening.
  - AMR (WHO): AMR happens when microbes "no longer respond to medicines intended to kill them"; misuse ("taking them when they are not needed or taking the wrong type") drives resistance (WHO antibiotic-resistance fact sheet, https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/antibiotic-resistance).
- **Not used:** a Malaysian figure, NHMS 2023 adult diabetes prevalence of 15.6%. News reports cite it, but I couldn't open the MOH Institute for Public Health fact sheet (iku.moh.gov.my didn't resolve from here). I'll only add it after someone opens the official PDF.
